What's the limit without any Perch involved
Hello everyone, I notice nearly all top-ranked notebooks on the leaderboard（including my own solutions,）rely heavily on Perch in one way or another. Most either extract audio embeddings via Perch or adopt Perch-based knowledge distillation. I think this may has caused severe lack of model diversity among our approaches. In my exps, merging another extra Perch-based model into ensembles barely brings any effective gain — the final LB improvement is only around 0.001 at most, which is basically within random score fluctuation. For this reason, I’ve been trying to train pure CNN branches only using mel-spectrogram input with EfficientNet-B0, without introducing any Perch weights, embeddings or distillation strategies at all. Unfortunately, all my attempts can hardly break 0.88 on public LB. Here are my two main questions:

1.What is the approximate upper bound public LB score we can reach if we stick purely to CNN pipelines with zero Perch involvement?
2.Under current mainstream ensemble frameworks, can we still obtain valid leaderboard gains by adding well-trained non-Perch models, even if their single-model scores are below 0.9?
Thanks a lot for all valuable discussions and opinions! 😊
